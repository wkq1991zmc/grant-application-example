# 03. Economics and Token

## Protocol Economics

The WORLD3 tokenomics model is engineered to foster a utility-driven, community-first, and sustainable economic ecosystem. Our design philosophy ensures alignment among all participants—developers, users, investors, and the core team—to drive collective growth.

1. Utility-Driven Value: The demand for the $WAI token is fundamentally linked to the core functionalities of the WORLD3 platform. It is more than a speculative asset—it is the essential fuel for operating AI Agents, accessing advanced features, and participating in governance. We believe that authentic platform usage is the ultimate source of token value.

2. Community-First Growth: We place our most vital contributors—the community—at the center of our ecosystem. Through significant airdrop incentives and a dedicated community growth fund, we reward early adopters, active users, and ecosystem builders, ensuring the benefits of platform growth are widely distributed.

3. Long-Term Commitment: Core contributors and early backers are subject to a multi-year vesting schedule with a significant cliff period. This structure ensures a deep, long-term alignment with the project's success, discouraging short-term speculation and providing a stable foundation for sustained development.

## Tokenomics Overview

The total supply of $WAI is capped at 1,000,000,000 (one billion) tokens. The allocation is strategically designed to support network operations, incentivize community engagement, and ensure market liquidity.

* **Supply Distribution:**

AI Agent Ecosystem: 47%
Powering the decentralized AI network and rewarding node operators

Airdrop Incentives: 16%
Rewarding early users and fostering broad community adoption.

Early Backers: 12%
Aligning with strategic investors and partners for long-term growth.

Core Contributors: 10%
Incentivizing the core team for sustained development and success.

Liquidity: 8%
Providing market depth and ensuring stable trading conditions.

Community Growth: 7%
Funding marketing, partnerships, and ecosystem initiatives.
